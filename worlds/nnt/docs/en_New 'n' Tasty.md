# Oddworld: New 'n' Tasty

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the options you need to configure and export a config file (but only if this is web hosted).

## What does randomisation do to this game?

This implementation shuffles various abilities (such as the ability to use Levers of Possess Sligs), area unlocks and Mudokons into the Archipelago item pool.

## What is the goal of Oddworld: New 'n' Tasty when randomised?

The goal of this randomisation is to rescue enough Mudokons to unlock either The Boardroom or the Alf's Escape DLC (depending on the player's settings). The required amount varies, defaulting to 50%.

## What items and locations get shuffled?

Area unlocks and abilities take up the initial item slots, with the remaining ones being filled by a variety of progression tagged Mudokons and filler Mudokons (which can optionally be replaced with various traps). Resucing Mudokons through Bird Portals will grant a location check for each Mudokon, and area clears can optionally be made into locations depending on the player's settings. Regardless of the area clear settings, each door in the Paramonian Temple, Scrabanian Temple, Zulag 2 and Zulag 3 are locations.

## Which items can be in another player's world?

Any shuffled item can be in other players' worlds.

## What does another world's item look like in Oddworld: New 'n' Tasty?

Items have no visual appearance in New 'n' Tasty due to the nature of the game's locations and difficulty in inserting custom assets into the game.

## When the player receives an item, what happens?

Receiving an item will display a plain text message at the bottom of the screen indicating what item the player received and its finder. Abilities can be used immediately, and traps will automatically activate if in a position to do so.
